For my first work term, I worked as an electrical engineering student at Irving Pulp and Paper. There, I worked in the Electrical & Instrumentation Reliability department, with project experience in the systems department. On the E&I reliability side, I completed work orders, ranging from equipment installation at the mill to preventative maintenance, control logic validation in the DCS, and mill improvement projects. I learned and employed AutoCAD to revise and create over 30 industrial loop diagrams, single line diagrams, and P&IDs to maintain engineering document accuracy. Additionally, I assembled 120 Motorola radios and wrote documentation to be used by the electrical and mechanical maintenance crews, as the mill was moving away from the pager system it previously used for communication. Before the end of my work term, I also wrote documentation for the HoloLens 2 by Microsoft.

In my systems projects, I worked on creating bi-directional HMIs that mill operators would use to monitor the O2 delignification and pulp washing processes at the mill, a project commissioned by BTG Voith. To achieve this, I first created the graphic that would be displayed in DeltaV Live in DeltaV Graphics Studio, and followed mill graphic design standards. The purpose of the HMIs was to allow operators greater visibility over the O2 Delig and Pulp Washing processes, and to automate variable setpoint changes. Each HMI had Manipulated Variables (MVs), and Control Variables (CVs).
